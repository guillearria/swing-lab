## Move
Merck jumped on a Phase 3 readout (8/18) — a mega-cap up double digits over 5d on ~3.7x volume,
with a wave of sell-side price-target hikes following.

## Why
Intismeran autogene (Moderna's personalized mRNA cancer vaccine) + Keytruda met its primary
recurrence-free-survival endpoint AND a key secondary (distant-metastasis-free survival) in
high-risk melanoma — the first positive Phase 3 for a personalized mRNA cancer therapy. It
does two things at once: adds a Keytruda-combo indication and, more importantly, validates a
pipeline that extends the franchise past Keytruda's looming patent cliff. Analysts re-rated
(Daiwa → Outperform, JPM PT $150).

## How
The structure: a mega-cap whose multiple was compressed by a single overhang (Keytruda LOE). A
binary readout that partially resolves that overhang lets the whole name re-rate, not just the
incremental drug's NPV — the re-rating is larger than the melanoma revenue alone would justify.

## Pattern (reusable)
**When one identifiable overhang caps a large-cap's multiple, the catalyst that resolves it
re-rates the whole name.** Spot it: a depressed mega-cap + a widely-cited single bear thesis +
a binary event that undercuts that thesis + confirming PT hikes. Slower and more durable than a
one-day earnings pop (63d, not 21d). Fails when the overhang is macro/structural (a readout
can't fix it) or the re-rating already fully happened intraday.

## Prediction
Long, 63d (durable franchise re-rating), vs XLV. Scored in `bets.py`.
