## Move
Estée Lauder gapped up on its fiscal-Q4 print (rpt 8/19) after a multi-year grind lower — a
double-digit one-day pop on heavy (~6x) volume, snapping a long losing streak.

## Why
Beat-and-raise: adj EPS beat, revenue +6%, organic +5% (fourth consecutive quarter of organic
growth), and — the drift fuel — a RAISED FY27 adjusted operating-margin outlook. Luxury
fragrance (Le Labo, Tom Ford) led. The market re-rated a turnaround it had left for dead.

## How
The structure that enables the drift: a washed-out name where consensus and positioning were
maximally bearish, so an upside inflection forces a slow repricing as estimates revise up and
underweight funds rebuild. The 5.9x relative volume is the tell that institutions are the ones
moving — not a retail one-day spike that fades.

## Pattern (reusable)
**Post-earnings drift on a beaten-down mega-cap re-rates over weeks, not one day** — when a
beat-and-raise arrives against maximally bearish positioning and heavy volume confirms
institutional repositioning, the immediate gap under-reflects the full estimate revision. Spot
it: multi-quarter downtrend + beat-and-RAISE (not just a beat) + >3x volume. Fails when the
"raise" is only in-line (no fresh upside to chase) or the name was already crowded-long.
