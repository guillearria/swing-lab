## Move
Iron Mountain showed clear relative weakness — down five sessions running while the broad
tape printed record highs — at a P/E far above its own five-year median, with heavy insider

## Why
A premium multiple is a claim about the future that must be continuously re-earned. When the
evidence stream turns — insider distribution, demand deceleration, analyst trims — the
multiple deflates toward the name's own history WITHOUT needing a single catalytic event.
Relative weakness against a strong tape is the tell: the marginal holder is already leaving.

## How
The structure that enables it: story multiples (data-center adjacency, compounder status)
attract momentum ownership that unwinds slowly, and mean reversion toward a five-year median
multiple is a long glide, not a gap. That is why both rows carry the long 63d horizon and a
broad-market benchmark: the claim is sustained UNDERperformance, not a crash.

## Pattern (reusable)
`valuation-deflation-short`: multiple far above the name's own history + evidence stream
turning (insiders, demand, PT cuts) + relative weakness against a strong tape → the premium
deflates over months. Spot it: P/E multiples of the 5yr median · insider sells with no buys ·
underperformance while the index rips. The traps this pattern owns: earnings inside the
window can whipsaw a valuation short (the IRM row's own stated event risk), and
deep-cheapness is the same trap mirrored — a capitulated "value" name can squeeze
