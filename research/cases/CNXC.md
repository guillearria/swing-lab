## Move
Concentrix fell ~a quarter of its value in one session on fiscal Q2: results roughly IN
LINE, but next-quarter and full-year guidance cut hard, double-digit percent below
consensus. The Trade Desk (TTD) is the corroborating instance: a revenue miss plus a guide

announced the same day.

## Why
This is the continuation read — the deliberate OPPOSITE of `guidance-cut-overreaction`
(`cases/MSCI.md`). There the numbers stayed intact and the price broke; here the FORWARD
numbers broke: a guide cut of this size means consensus estimates are still too high even
after the drop, and post-earnings drift on a genuine negative surprise continues in the
direction of the surprise. "Cheap" after such a cut is a value trap while estimates are
still falling; management turmoil (TTD) is confirmation the guide-down is structural.

## How
The structure that enables it: sell-side models re-anchor to a cut guide over weeks, not in
one session, so the first drop under-prices the full revision cycle. A secular overhang (AI
displacement of the business model, in both anchors) caps any multiple rebuild, removing the
mean-reversion force the overreaction pattern relies on.

## Pattern (reusable)
`guidance-cut-continuation`: results in line or worse PLUS a forward guide cut large enough
to imply consensus still hasn't caught down → the drift continues lower; the dip is not
buyable. Spot it: guide cut ≥~10% below consensus (not a cents-level trim) · a sequential
decline implied at a "growth" name · executive turnover landing with the print · a secular
story that explains WHY estimates keep falling. The discriminator vs `cases/MSCI.md` is the
LOCUS of the damage: expenses/one segment with demand intact = overreaction candidate;
forward revenue/EPS guide broken = continuation. The trap this pattern owns: capitulation at
a single-digit multiple invites squeezes and dead-cat bounces — both anchor rows carry
exactly that stated risk, at LOW/MEDIUM conviction.
